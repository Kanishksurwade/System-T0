# Day 1: The Idea & Handshake AI Challenge

Today I started my journey to build "System: T-0", a gamified productivity app inspired by anime (like Solo Leveling). 

**What I did:**
* Conceptualized a 4K glassmorphic lock screen widget for the Handshake AI November challenge.
* Analyzed competitors like Habitica, Todoist, and Minimalist. Found a gap: they either have boring UIs or messy setups. 
* Decided on an offline-first strategy to keep the app fast and server costs at $0.
* Locked in the core mechanics: tasks act as "Quests" that grant gold and XP when ticked off.
