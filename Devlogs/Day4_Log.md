# Day 4: Design Complete — Time to Build

## 1. The Big Milestone

Today I finished **100% of the UI designs in Canva**. Every major screen is now mapped out:

- Profile
- Goals
- Calendar
- Shop
- Premium
- Settings
- Widget

That gives me a complete visual blueprint to work from instead of figuring out the interface while coding.

## 2. The Aesthetic

The visual direction is locked in: a premium, dark glassmorphic interface with a **Solo Leveling-inspired anime vibe**.

Think deep blacks, layered glass panels, sharp highlights, and a sense of progression that makes everyday productivity feel like a quest.

## 3. The Decision: Quality Comes First

I spent time stressing over platform fees, but I finally made the call: I'm accepting the **$99 Apple Developer fee** and committing to building System: T-0 natively for iOS with **SwiftUI**.

It's a real cost, but I would rather invest in the platform and experience I want to deliver than compromise the product just to avoid the fee. Quality comes first.

## 4. The Smart Strategy

Finishing every screen in Canva before writing the app is deliberate. The designs are now the reference, so I can spend my **Claude 3.5 Sonnet tokens** on actual code generation tomorrow rather than burning them deciding layouts, spacing, and visual details.

The goal is to move into implementation with fewer unknowns and a clear target.
